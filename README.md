# Financial Data Reconciliation Workflow

This repository contains the workflow, documentation, and reconciliation tables for **AGSM & Non‑AGSM Collections and Transfers**.  
It is designed to provide a transparent view of collections, transfers, shortfalls, adjustments, and outstanding balances in a structured format.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
The goal of this project is to:
- Track **AGSM and Non‑AGSM cash collections**.
- Reconcile **bank deposits, Dokilink remittances, and Eco Bank transfers**.
- Highlight **shortfalls, excess transfers, and BOT dues**.
- Present clear scenarios for **net outstanding balances owed by Dakar AGSM**.

This workflow ensures that both accounting professionals and non‑accounting stakeholders can understand the reconciliation process.

---

## 📂 Contents
- **Statement of Income & Expenditure**  
  High‑level snapshot of collections and payments.

- **AGSM & Non‑AGSM Reconciliation Summary**  
  Detailed breakdown of variances, shortfalls, BOT dues, and adjustments.

- **Scenario Analysis**  
  - **Scenario A:** Dakar owes **20,062** (including Dokilink shortfall).  
  - **Scenario B:** Dakar owes **15,298** (excluding Dokilink shortfall already given to Dokilink).  

---

## 🛠️ Workflow Highlights
- **Collections:** AGSM deposits, Non‑AGSM deposits, Dokilink remittances.  
- **Transfers:** Eco Bank and LIKAK transfers.  
- **Adjustments:** Cash received from College President, BOT dues, Non‑AGSM excess.  
- **Outstanding Balances:** Presented in both scenarios for clarity.

---

## ✅ Usage
This repository can be used to:
- Review reconciliation tables for financial reporting.
- Share transparent financial workflows with stakeholders.
- Provide both **summary** and **detailed reconciliation views** in one place.

---

## 📌 Notes
- Figures are based on reconciled data as of October 2025.  
- The reconciliation includes both **AGSM** and **Non‑AGSM** flows.  
- The dual‑scenario approach ensures clarity on whether the Dokilink shortfall is treated as Dakar’s liability or excluded.
